# AgentForge requirement-to-evidence register

Source: the local [original 18-page AgentForge PRD](../../docs/Week%203_AgentForge_PRD.pdf), read for this B01 revision; target guarantees come from the later [Co-Pilot feature brief](../../docs/COPILOT_FEATURE.md). This is a **rubric-first tracking register**, not a declaration of submission completion. Baseline evidence is the September 28 [audit at `e1ae270`](../../docs/audit/2026-09-28/AUDIT.md) and the current checkout; B01 documents are new in this branch. `Implemented` means directly supported by the linked current code/test or artifact for the narrow claim. `Partial` means working mechanism with an identified gap. `Planned` means an owned issue, without implementation proof. `External gate` requires operator or independent-party evidence and cannot become complete from a fixture pass. Original PRD labels “Optional Engineering Deliverables” but explicitly says they are graded and “not optional”; this register tracks them as required.

The [delivery plan](../implementation/audit-delivery/PLAN.md) and [task manifest](../implementation/audit-delivery/tasks.json) provide full issue scope and dependencies. Linked Linear issues are **owners**, not evidence of completion. The auditor's single-operator LAN access does not satisfy the PRD's public deployed **target** requirement. All attacks remain synthetic and user-facing; the optional approved read-only adapter is evidence-only and disabled by default. Chart-context isolation replaces obsolete reciprocal chart-access-denial wording.

Remote CI is **pending**: the coordinator reports the known runner `281` has never contacted GitLab and shared runners are off. Local checks can validate a revision temporarily, but no green remote pipeline or automated CI gate is claimed. [Q03 / HDX-40](https://linear.app/hdxsfbr/issue/HDX-40) owns runner recovery; [D01 / HDX-41](https://linear.app/hdxsfbr/issue/HDX-41) owns coordinator policy. Neither is silently counted as rubric completion.
